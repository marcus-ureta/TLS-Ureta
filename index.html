Overview

Marshmallow Boy is a 2D platformer game, similar to Celeste. Marshmallow Boy finds himself having to traverse through treacherous terrain to reach Marshmallow Land. 

Story

You are Marshmallow Boy, a cute fluffy marshmallow fellow who yearns for a land he cannot reach. Until one day a strange cookie appeared and offered to show the way there. Now it's up to you, can you get to Marshmallow Land?
