MARSHMALLOW BOY

Overview

Marshmallow Boy is a 2D platformer game, similar to Celeste. Marshmallow Boy finds himself having to traverse through treacherous terrain to reach Marshmallow Land. 

Story

You are Marshmallow Boy, a cute fluffy marshmallow fellow who yearns for a land he cannot reach. Until one day a strange cookie appeared and offered to show the way there. Now it's up to you, can you get to Marshmallow Land?  

Game Features

Complex Movement Capabilities

As Marshmallow Boy, you're built for speed and precision with your movement capabilities. As a tiny, petite, and squishy marshmallow, you have the ability to dash across large gaps! Furthermore, while you're in the dash state, Marshmallow Boy can use his momentum to double jump. To get vertical height, Marshmallow Boy can also climb walls!

Collectibles

The journey to Marshmallow Land isn't all just spikes and lava! Across each level, you may come across marshmallow collectibles that you can collect. These collectibles can be used to unlock cosmetics, achievements, and even one bonus level!

Modern Pixel Art World

The world of Marshmallow Boy is a blend between modernity and pixel-art. It uses pixel-art to portray the world and characters around them, however the world shaped around them is based off modern design elements.
